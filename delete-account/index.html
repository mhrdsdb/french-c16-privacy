ACCOUNT & DATA REQUESTS

Delete your French C16 account

You can request deletion of your French C16 account and its associated personal learning information even if the Android app is no longer installed.

How to request deletion

Contact the developer at mhrdsdb@gmail.com from the email associated with your French C16 account if possible.

Suggested subject: French C16 — Delete my account

Email a deletion request

In your message, include only your registered email address and, optionally, username, and explicitly say you want your French C16 account and associated data deleted. Never include your password, authentication token or verification code.

We may ask you to verify account ownership before deleting anything to prevent unauthorized deletion.

What the request covers

  • Registered account information, email and username.
  • Login sessions and authentication tokens associated with the account.
  • Individual study history, completed tasks, timers, notes, assessment scores and progress stored for the account.

We will respond to your request and confirm the outcome. Information may be retained only when there is a legitimate security or legal reason, which we will explain. Backup copies may have a separate retention lifecycle.

Delete study data only

You may also email us if you want particular study information removed without closing your account. Specify the information you would like deleted.
