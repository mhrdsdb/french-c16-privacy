Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the French C16 Android app and its connected learning dashboard handle your personal information.

Effective date: October 10, 2026
Developer / operator: mhrdsdb
Contact: mhrdsdb@gmail.com

1. Information we handle

When you use the app or create a French C16 account, the service may handle:

  • Account information: your email address, username, password hash, verification and recovery-related records, and account status.
  • Authentication and security: session tokens stored as hashes on the server, token activity timestamps, and technical request details needed to help protect the service.
  • Learning information: study schedules, assigned activities, task completion, study timers, notes, listening/speaking scores, and progress statistics.
  • Preferences: app language, your configured dashboard server address, and sign-in session information stored on your device.
  • Network data: your IP address and technical connection details may be processed by the service's network infrastructure to deliver and secure requests.

The current Android application requests internet/network access. Its app manifest does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.

2. How the information is used

  • Create and secure user accounts, verify email addresses, and assist with password recovery.
  • Display your learning plan and maintain study progress across sessions.
  • Run study timers, save notes, and report progress.
  • Synchronize shared study-plan data from the developer's private GitHub repository.
  • Maintain service security, diagnose problems, and respond to support or deletion requests.

We do not sell your personal information. The current app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Hosting, service providers and external links

The French C16 dashboard is operated on a Docker-based server. Its HTTPS connectivity may be delivered through ngrok, which can process connection-related technical information. Email verification and recovery messages are sent using an email delivery provider (currently SMTP/Gmail). Shared study plans may be downloaded from a private GitHub repository; this is separate from individual account records and progress databases.

Links to external learning services (such as Busuu, TV5MONDE or ChatGPT) may open those providers' sites or applications, which have their own policies. We do not claim to control their data practices.

Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect the service, subject to applicable law.

4. Where information is stored and how it is protected

Account and study data are stored in server-side SQLite databases. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than plaintext, session authentication is protected, and the Android app uses encrypted local preferences for its authentication session. HTTPS is used when connecting to the configured public dashboard.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Keep your password private and use only a dashboard server you trust.

5. Retention and your choices

Account and learning information are retained while needed to operate your account, provide the service, meet legitimate security needs, or comply with applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account information by contacting us.

Request account and data deletion

See our dedicated deletion instructions. We may verify account ownership before acting. The account and associated study data will be deleted in response to a verified request unless specific information must be retained for a legitimate legal or security reason; any such exception will be explained.

Account deletion instructions

Historical backup copies may require additional time to cycle out; contact us for information about any backup-related retention affecting your request.

6. Children and policy changes

French C16 is designed for independent adult language learners, not specifically for children. If you believe a minor's information was provided improperly, contact us for assistance.

We may update this policy as the app or service changes. The effective date at the top of this page will show when this policy was last updated.

7. Contact

For privacy, data access, corrections, deletion requests, or security questions, email mhrdsdb@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or verification codes by email.
